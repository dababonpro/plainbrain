# The PARA method

## Purpose
Why notes here carry a `para` field, and what each value means.

## Summary
Tiago Forte's PARA method sorts information by how actionable it is, not by subject:
- **Project:** work with a goal and an end.
- **Area:** a responsibility you keep up over time, with no end date.
- **Resource:** a topic you are interested in or may need later.
- **Archive:** anything from the other three that is no longer active.

The idea is that you look for information when you act, so it should sit near the action it serves.

## How you use it
Forte uses folders. Here PARA is a field instead, so a note never has to move when its project ends: you change `para` to `archive`. The PARA views show each group. Archive means not active; it does not mean wrong, which is what `status: superseded` is for. Your commitments stay in the commitment list, not in project notes. See [[running-a-second-brain]].

## Limits
This is a summary of the method in our words, not Forte's text. His article and book explain it in full.
