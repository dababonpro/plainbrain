# Running a second brain

## Purpose
How the parts of this second brain fit together, so you know where each thing goes.

## Useful information
- **Capture** anything worth keeping into the inbox as one line, from wherever you are. Do not sort it yet.
- **Commitments** live in one list: what you owe, what you are waiting on, and the next action for each. The daily brief reads it and suggests what to do next.
- **Notes** hold knowledge worth finding again: decisions, references, procedures, lessons and techniques. Each says where it came from and when.
- **The weekly review** turns inbox lines into commitments or notes, and drops the rest. See [[weekly-review]].
- **Three fields** describe each note. `type` says what kind of knowledge it is, `para` says how actionable it is (see [[para-method]]), and `status` says whether it is still true. They answer different questions, so a note can be a current technique filed under resources.
- **An agent** can search, answer from and update these notes. It cites the note for every claim, and asks before changing a note you did not ask it to change.

## Rationale or verification
Keeping commitments and knowledge apart stops a note from quietly becoming a to-do list that nothing reminds you of. The commitment list is reviewed daily; notes are found when needed.

## Limits
This describes the starting setup. Change it when your own use shows a better way, and record why in a decision note.
