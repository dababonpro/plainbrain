# Weekly review

## Purpose
How to keep the inbox empty and the commitment list true, in about thirty minutes a week.

## Useful information
1. Run the weekly review command. It lists stale commitments, waiting items past their follow-up date, and what is in the inbox.
2. For each inbox line, decide one thing: make it a commitment, add it to a note, or drop it. A line with no next action and no lasting value is dropped.
3. For each waiting item past its follow-up date, follow up or change the date.
4. Pick up to three commitments for the week, and one focus.
5. Run the guided researcher on your notes once a month, and decide its proposals.

## Rationale or verification
It works when the inbox is empty at the end and every active commitment has a next action you could start today. If the review keeps running over an hour, capture less or review twice a week.

## Limits
Thirty minutes assumes a few dozen inbox lines. A busy week needs longer.
